# Glossary

The names we use for each part of the screen, so we always mean the same thing.

## Always on screen

- **Bar**: the vertical strip on the left edge. From top to bottom it holds:
  - **Y**: the Yui logo; click it to open Spotlight.
  - **Workspace dots**: one dot per workspace (virtual desktop); the highlighted one is where
    you are.
  - **Window title**: the name of the active window, written vertically.
  - **Tray**: icons of apps running in the background (Discord, Steam...).
  - **Clock**.
  - **Status icons**: network, Bluetooth, battery, keyboard layout and similar. Hovering one
    opens its **popout**.
  - **Power button**: opens the session menu.
- **Dock**: the app strip at the bottom centre.
- **Title bar**: the strip on top of each window with its name and the three **window buttons**
  (close, minimize, zoom).
- **Wallpaper**: the desktop picture; the system colours come from it.

## Things that appear when needed

- **Popout**: a small card that grows out of the bar next to the item it belongs to (network,
  Bluetooth, sound, battery, tray menus).
- **Dashboard**: the panel that drops from the top edge, with tabs for the clock and calendar,
  media and performance.
- **Sidebar**: the panel on the right edge with notifications (`Super+N`).
- **Utilities**: the small panel in the bottom-right corner (quick toggles, recording).
- **Notification**: the card that pops up in the corner when an app sends a message.
- **OSD** (on-screen display): the slider that appears on the right when you change the volume
  or brightness.
- **Session menu**: lock, log out, restart, shut down.
- **Lock screen**.

## Overlays

Full-screen layers that sit above everything until you choose something:

- **Spotlight**: the search box.
- **Switcher**: the row of window previews of Alt+Tab.
- **Mission Control**: every window of the screen laid out at once.
- **Snap preview**: the outline showing where a window will land while you drag it to an edge.

## Apps of the system

- **Settings**: the settings window, with **pages** listed on the left (Displays, Network...).
- **Terminal**: Ghostty.
